HORAЕ MERCHANT SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY
Policy Version: 2026.1

1. ORDER FULFILLMENT

Orders are processed using the shipping address supplied by the customer during checkout.

The merchant records the order timestamp, fulfillment timestamp, shipping destination, carrier information, and tracking identifier.

2. DELIVERY CONFIRMATION

Carrier tracking information is considered a primary fulfillment record.

A delivery event containing a successful delivery status, delivery timestamp, and destination information may be used as evidence when responding to an ITEM_NOT_RECEIVED dispute.

3. DELIVERY EXCEPTIONS

If tracking indicates an attempted delivery, address issue, carrier exception, or returned shipment, the merchant reviews the shipment before determining whether a refund is appropriate.

4. CUSTOMER-PROVIDED ADDRESS

Customers are responsible for providing an accurate shipping address during checkout.

Where the order was shipped to the address supplied by the customer and carrier records confirm delivery, the merchant may use these records when contesting a non-delivery claim.

5. RECORD RETENTION

Shipping and fulfillment records are retained alongside the associated transaction identifier where available.

Relevant records may include tracking numbers, fulfillment timestamps, delivery status, and carrier events.
