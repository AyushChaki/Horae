HORAЕ MERCHANT REFUND POLICY
Policy Version: 2026.1

1. GENERAL REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Customers may request a refund within 7 calendar days of confirmed delivery for eligible physical products.

Refunds are not automatically approved solely because a customer claims dissatisfaction. The merchant may request supporting information and verify the condition of the returned item.

2. ITEM NOT RECEIVED

For an ITEM_NOT_RECEIVED claim, the merchant may provide shipment tracking information, carrier delivery confirmation, delivery timestamps, and proof of delivery.

Where the carrier confirms successful delivery to the shipping address provided during checkout, the merchant may contest an unsupported non-delivery claim.

3. DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE PRODUCTS

Customers reporting a defective or damaged product must provide photographic or other reasonable evidence when requested.

Where applicable, the merchant may request return of the product for inspection before approving a refund.

4. PRODUCT SWAPPED OR RETURNED IN DIFFERENT CONDITION

Returned products may be inspected against the original fulfillment record.

If the returned item differs materially from the item shipped, contains missing components, or shows evidence inconsistent with the reported issue, the merchant may withhold or partially adjust the refund subject to applicable consumer protection requirements.

5. REFUND PROCESSING

Approved refunds are normally initiated within 5 business days after the return or claim has been verified.

The merchant retains transaction, fulfillment, and refund records to support dispute investigation.

6. SUBSCRIPTION CANCELLATION AND REFUNDS

For a SUBSCRIPTION_CANCELLED_REFUND dispute, the merchant may review the subscription status, cancellation timestamp, billing records, payment records, and refund records.

A cancellation request does not by itself establish that a refund is due. The merchant may verify whether the subscription was successfully cancelled, whether a charge occurred before or after cancellation, and whether the applicable refund conditions were satisfied.

Evidence may include cancellation timestamps, subscription status history, billing events, payment transaction records, customer communications, and refund processing records.

Where a refund was approved, the merchant may provide the associated refund record and processing information when responding to the dispute.

7. REFUND RECORD RETENTION

The merchant retains transaction, fulfillment, subscription, cancellation, and refund records where available to support dispute investigation.
