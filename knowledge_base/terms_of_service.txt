HORAЕ MERCHANT TERMS OF SERVICE
Policy Version: 2026.1

1. CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITY

Customers must provide accurate billing, shipping, and account information when placing an order.

2. ACCOUNT SECURITY

Customers are responsible for maintaining the security of their account credentials and should promptly report suspected unauthorized activity.

3. TRANSACTION RECORDS

The merchant maintains records associated with transactions, including transaction identifiers, order details, payment information, fulfillment information, and relevant customer communications.

4. UNAUTHORIZED TRANSACTIONS

For an UNAUTHORIZED_TRANSACTION dispute, the merchant may review available authentication, account, device, payment, and transaction records.

Evidence may include transaction timestamps, account activity, device information, authentication records, and other available transaction metadata.

5. DISPUTE REVIEW

Dispute responses should be supported by relevant transaction records and applicable merchant policies.

The merchant may contest a dispute where available evidence contradicts the stated dispute reason, subject to applicable payment-network and consumer-protection requirements.
