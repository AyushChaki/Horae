HORAЕ MERCHANT RETURN POLICY
Policy Version: 2026.1

1. RETURN WINDOW

Eligible physical products may generally be returned within 7 calendar days from confirmed delivery, subject to product-specific restrictions.

2. CONDITION OF RETURNED ITEMS

Returned products should be in a condition consistent with the approved return reason.

The merchant may inspect returned items for damage, missing accessories, product substitution, or material differences from the original fulfillment record.

3. PRODUCT DEFECT CLAIMS

For PRODUCT_DEFECTIVE_OR_SWAPPED claims, the merchant may compare the returned product against fulfillment records and request supporting evidence.

Where inspection confirms that the returned item does not correspond to the fulfilled product, the merchant may contest the claim.

4. NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS

Certain digital goods, personalized products, and other explicitly identified categories may be excluded from standard physical-product returns.

5. REFUND APPROVAL

A return request does not itself constitute approval of a refund.

Refunds are processed after eligibility and, where applicable, returned-product verification.

6. PRODUCT NOT AS DESCRIBED

For a NOT_AS_DESCRIBED dispute, the merchant may compare the product supplied against the product description, listing information, order details, SKU, specifications, and original fulfillment records.

Evidence may include the product listing shown at the time of purchase, product specifications, SKU information, fulfillment records, photographs, customer communications, and returned-product inspection results.

Where the fulfilled product corresponds materially to the product information and order details provided at the time of purchase, the merchant may use those records when responding to a NOT_AS_DESCRIBED dispute.

Where a material difference is identified between the product supplied and the applicable product description or order information, the merchant should review the claim and available return or inspection evidence before determining the appropriate resolution.

7. PRODUCT INFORMATION RECORDS

The merchant may retain product descriptions, SKU information, fulfillment records, product specifications, and relevant customer communications to support investigation of product-related disputes.
